# SOP-07.3 rev4 — Rotating Equipment Vibration Response

**Applies to:** all monitored rotating equipment, criticality medium and above.
**Owner:** Maintenance Standards. **Effective:** 2026-05-22.

## 4. Response thresholds

| Condition | Required response | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10% above baseline | continue routine monitoring | routine |
| 10-25% above baseline | daily monitoring + corrective work order | within 72 h |
| Above alarm limit | reduce load, notify shift supervisor | immediate |
| Above trip limit | controlled shutdown per unit procedure | immediate |

### 4.2 Daily monitoring requirement

When overall vibration exceeds baseline by more than 10%, monitoring frequency
shall be increased to once per shift, and a corrective work order shall be
raised within 72 hours. The work order must reference the inspection report
that identified the deviation.

### 4.3 Bearing inspection trigger

A drive-end bearing inspection is required when either condition is met:

* overall vibration exceeds baseline by 15% or more with 1x dominance, or
* bearing housing temperature rises more than 10 degC above baseline.

### 5. Approval

Work orders that require an equipment outage on criticality-high equipment
require Maintenance Manager approval before scheduling.
