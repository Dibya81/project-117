# SOP-11.2 — Lockout / Tagout for Rotating Equipment

**Owner:** Safety. **Effective:** 2026-01-30.

1. Obtain a work permit referencing the work order number.
2. Isolate electrical supply at the designated disconnect; apply personal lock.
3. Isolate process suction and discharge; bleed to atmospheric pressure.
4. Verify zero energy state (electrical, pressure, stored rotational energy).
5. Attach tags recording permit number, time, and responsible person.
6. Removal of locks requires the same person who applied them, or a documented
   supervisor override with two signatures.
